# Expense Policy  

Effective 2026-03-01. Owner: Finance.  

## What can be claimed  

Business travel, client meals, training approved by the team lead, and home office equipment up to 40 EUR per year (keyboard, mouse, headset).  

## Limits  

| Category | Limit | Approval |  
|---|---|---|  
| Hotel, per night | 120 EUR | Team lead |  
| Meals, per day | 45 EUR | Not needed |  
| Training | 600 EUR per year | Team lead and HR |  
| Taxi | 30 EUR per trip | Not needed |  

## How to submit  

Upload the receipt to the expense tool within 30 days. Claims without a receipt are rejected. Reimbursement is paid with the next salary.  

## Not reimbursed  

Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment and upgrades to business class.
