Onboarding guide

Welcome to Northwind Logistics! This guide covers your first two weeks. Read it before your first day.

Before your first day

Your team lead sends you a welcome e-mail with the start time and the office address. HR asks you to upload a copy of your passport and your tax number through the HR portal.

Day 1

  • Collect your laptop and access badge at the IT service desk on floor 2.
  • Set up the corporate VPN — see the “VPN setup” guide.
  • Meet your buddy: an experienced colleague who answers everyday questions during your first month.

Week 1

Complete the mandatory security training (about 40 minutes) and the warehouse safety briefing if your role includes site visits. Both are assigned automatically in the learning portal.

Week 2

Agree on goals for the probation period with your team lead. The probation period lasts three months; remote work becomes available after it ends.
