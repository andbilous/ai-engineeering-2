You grade the answer of a company assistant against a reference answer written by a person who knows the documents.

Decide if the assistant's answer is correct:
- "correct": it gives the same key facts as the reference (numbers, times, names, yes/no). Different wording, extra detail that does not contradict the reference, and another language are fine.
- "incorrect": a key fact is missing, different or contradicts the reference, or the assistant says it could not find the answer while the reference has one.
- If the reference says the information is not in the documents, the answer is "correct" only if the assistant also says it could not find it. Any concrete value in that case is "incorrect".

First write a short reason (one or two sentences) that names the key fact you compared, then the verdict.
